// Who I am

Two disciplines, one operator.

Most people pick engineering or business. I chose both — a BTech in Chemical Engineering from Nirma University, then an MS in Technology Management at Gies, UIUC (3.9 GPA). The combination is the point: I can sit with a process and with a P&L in the same afternoon.

Before grad school I spent 4+ years running real operations at a B2B manufacturing & trading firm — managing vendor relationships, tracking inventory across product lines, analyzing sales data, and building a client pipeline from scratch with data-driven outreach across six PVA grades.

Since then it's been forecasting models, case competitions, and AI agents — things that ship, not slideware. I'm targeting roles that need someone who can read a dataset, talk to an engineer, and present to a VP without losing anyone in the room.
